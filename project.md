For Senior Project II, develop an open-source AI agent, demonstrate a working prototype, and report how you evaluated it and what you found.

## Final project deadlines

| Date (Fall 2026) | What to submit or attend |
| --- | --- |
| December 8 by 11:59 p.m. Eastern | Submit final presentation slides with visualizations of your project. |
| December 9 and 14, in class | Attend both sessions of the final research project presentations. Attendance is mandatory for all students on both days. |
| December 15 by 11:59 p.m. Eastern | Submit the eight-page final research report and your final code, data, and artifacts. |

## Research report

Write an **eight-page, single-column LaTeX report** using the [course style file](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nEb6n5z49fTlBe9nu2Lhgm7TYhjZoGnk/view?usp=sharing). Describe your methods, evaluation, and findings. Cite your sources in the text and place the reference list after the eight pages. You may put additional diagrams in an appendix outside the page limit. If your team needs an alternative to LaTeX, contact the professor promptly.

## Code, data, and artifacts

Submit documented, open-source project code along with your final data and artifacts. Include a link to the code in your report and identify its license.

## Project milestones and grading

The group project accounts for 53% of the course grade. The one-page abstract is worth 5%, the midterm presentation 7%, the final presentation 10%, and the final report 31%. In each of the five research progress updates, describe your individual contributions and the work completed jointly.

See [Assignments]({{ '/assignments/' | relative_url }}) for milestone dates and the [course syllabus]({{ '/syllabus/' | relative_url }}) for the full requirements.
