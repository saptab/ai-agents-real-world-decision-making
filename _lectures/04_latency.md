**Key Topics:**
* **High-Throughput Orchestration:** Multi-agent GPU training via JAXMARL, durable DAG workflows in Ray, and hardware-agnostic control in PyLabRobot.
* **Reward Hacking Case Study:** Specification gaming in peptide self-assembly via evaporative heating.
* **Policy Regularization:** Gravimetric mass loss balance, dual-wavelength IR scattering, and Out-of-Distribution (OOD) action masking.
* **Computation vs. Communication Tradeoffs:** Communication Penalty ($CP = T_{\text{TOTAL}} / T_{\text{COMP}}$) and speedup limits.
* **Point-to-Point Link Latency:** Asynchronous packet delay modeling ($D = P + RL + Q$) across processing, channel capacity, and buffer contention.
* **Pipelined Transmission:** Segmenting payloads to overlap transmission delay across multi-hop paths ($T_{\text{pipelined}} = (n + k - 1)T$) and cut-through routing.
